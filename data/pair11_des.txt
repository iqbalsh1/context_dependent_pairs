Time-Dependent Pair11 Description

This dataset contains 1500 samples with a temperature-dependent causal relationship between variables X and Y.
Temperature is uniformly sampled from the range [-5°C, 20°C].

A causal shift occurs at the threshold T = 8°C:

Segment 1 (T < 8°C):
    Causal Direction: X → Y
    Generation:
        X ~ λ · 𝒩(−3, 1.0²) + (1−λ) · 𝒩(8, 2.5²), with λ = 0.5
        Y = 2.0 · X + ε,     ε ~ Laplace(0, 1.0)

Segment 2 (T ≥ 8°C):
    Causal Direction: Y → X
    Generation:
        Y ~ λ · 𝒩(−3, 1.0²) + (1−λ) · 𝒩(8, 2.5²), with λ = 0.5
        X = −1.5 · Y + ε,     ε ~ Laplace(0, 1.0)

Noise terms are sampled from a Laplace distribution with mean 0 and scale 1.0.
Regime labels are not provided. The causal direction changes at T = 8°C.
