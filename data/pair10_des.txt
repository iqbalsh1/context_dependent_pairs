Time-Dependent Pair10 Description

This dataset contains 1000 samples with a temperature-dependent causal relationship between variables X and Y.
Temperature is uniformly sampled from the range [−10°C, 30°C].

A causal shift occurs at the threshold T = 15°C:

Segment 1 (T < 15°C):
    Causal Direction: X → Y
    Generation:
        X ~ λ · 𝒩(−8, 1.5²) + (1−λ) · 𝒩(12, 3.0²), with λ = 0.7
        Y = 1.8 · X + ε,     ε ~ 𝒩(0, 1.5²)

Segment 2 (T ≥ 15°C):
    Causal Direction: Y → X
    Generation:
        Y ~ λ · 𝒩(−8, 1.5²) + (1−λ) · 𝒩(12, 3.0²), with λ = 0.7
        X = −2.2 · Y + ε,     ε ~ 𝒩(0, 1.5²)

Noise is drawn from a Gaussian distribution with mean 0 and standard deviation 1.5.
Regime labels are not included. The causal direction changes at T = 15°C.

Random seed: 42
